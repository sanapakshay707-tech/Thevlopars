# Demo Script (about 5 minutes)

1. **Problem (30 sec):** Judgments are long and hard to read. Clerks, students, and citizens lose time finding the key points and related cases.
2. **Upload (30 sec):** Upload one prepared judgment PDF.
3. **Analysis (1 min):** Show category, parties, Acts/sections, legal issue, facts, outcome and summary. Click one item to show the source passage and page number.
4. **Similar cases (1.5 min):** Show top 5. For each: score, shared provisions, why it matched, stored outcome, source link.
5. **Honesty (1 min):** Show an uncertain/"Not found" field. Say: scores are research leads, not legal conclusions. The dataset is a small sample.
6. **Wrap up (30 sec):** Local processing, evidence-first design, future work (OCR, bigger dataset, legal-specific models).

Backup plan: keep a screen recording and a second PDF ready. Test the full demo on the demo laptop, with Wi-Fi off.
Use real judgments with verified metadata. Label any illustrative data clearly.
